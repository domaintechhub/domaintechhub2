### Q4: How does Safaricom Daraja 3.0 M-Pesa STK Push work on your websites?
**A**: We build direct API connections to Safaricom's Daraja 3.0 gateway. At checkout, the customer enters their Safaricom phone number and clicks "Pay with M-Pesa". A native STK prompt pops up instantly on their phone screen requesting their M-Pesa PIN. Once entered, Safaricom fires an asynchronous webhook callback to our backend, which instantly updates order status and dispatches SMS receipts.
