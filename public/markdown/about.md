# About Domain Tech Hub — Nairobi Web Engineering Studio

## Who We Are
Domain Tech Hub is an established digital technology and software engineering agency based in Nairobi, Kenya (Delta Corner Tower, Westlands). ## Our Philosophy: Engineering Over Templates
Traditional agencies often outsource client projects to unvetted freelancers or slap together bloated WordPress themes with 30+ plugins. These sites crash under high M-Pesa transaction volumes, load in 4+ seconds, and fail Google search rankings.

We took the opposite engineering approach:
1. **Strict TypeScript & Clean Code**: Predictable, type-safe codebases without runtime surprises.
2. **Sub-Second Performance**: Largest Contentful Paint under 1.0 second on mobile 4G networks across Kenya.
3. **Native African Payment DNA**: Direct API-level integration with Safaricom Daraja 3.0 M-Pesa STK Push.
4. **Complete Client Ownership**: 100% IP, Git repository commit history, and database access transferred to the client.
5. **Predictable Fixed-Bid Sprints**: 4 to 8 week milestones with signed agreements and zero surprise invoices.
